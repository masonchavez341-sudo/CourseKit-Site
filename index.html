CourseKit

Pace notes for desert racing. Open the waypoint files from a Lowrance GPS, see them on a map with race miles, clean them up, and write a file that loads straight back onto the unit.

  • Edit notes: Lowrance .usr, GPX, KML and KMZ files, a roadbook list in race-mile order, your own pace-note rules, automatic corner calls (L1–L6 or L 45–L 180).
  • Record a drive: say “waypoint” and the note while pre-running; the phone drops the pin where you said it.

Your files never leave your device.

Open CourseKit

Put it on your iPhone home screen

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open CourseKit.
  3. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open it from the new CourseKit icon. It works without signal once it has been opened once.
