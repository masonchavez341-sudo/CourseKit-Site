CourseKit

Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · Published by Mason Chavez

CourseKit edits GPS waypoint files and records pace notes. It is built so that nothing that identifies you is sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. We (the developer) receive no information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

Files you open, the waypoints and notes you edit, your recorded drives (audio, transcript, GPS track and waypoints) and your settings are processed and stored only on your device. Files you export go wherever you choose to save or send them; we never see them.

Location and microphone

CourseKit asks for your location and microphone only when you use Record a drive or show your position on the map. Your location is used on the device to place waypoints and draw your track. Audio is recorded to your device.

Speech-to-text is provided by your device's operating system. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process speech on its servers under Apple's privacy policy.

Map backgrounds

When a map background is switched on, the app requests the map squares for the area on screen directly from these providers:

  • Esri: satellite imagery
  • OpenTopoMap: topographic map
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: street map

As with any internet request, they can see your device's IP address and the map area requested, and handle it under their own privacy policies. The requests contain no personal information from the app. Turn the background off to make no such requests.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
